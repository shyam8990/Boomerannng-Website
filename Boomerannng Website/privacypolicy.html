Privacy Policy

For visitors, finders and registered users of boomerannng.com Last updated: 01 Nov 2026
1. Introduction
Boomerannng respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Website, why we collect it, how we protect it and what choices you have. It is written to comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 (together, “Data Protection Law”). For the mobile app, see the Parent App Privacy Policy.
We are the Data Fiduciary for personal data processed through the Website.

2. Personal data we collect

CategoryExamplesWhen we collect it
Contact details Name, email, phone number, message content You complete a contact, demo or newsletter form
Account details Name, email, phone number, password (stored in hashed form) You register
Finder scan data Date and time of scan, Tag identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, any message you choose to send You scan a Tag
Device and usage data IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring page, cookie identifiers You visit the Website
Support records Correspondence and order references You contact us

We do not collect payment card numbers on the Website. Payments are handled by our payment partners. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the Website.

3. How we use personal data
To operate the Website, display Scan Pages and deliver “Notify owner” messages.
To respond to enquiries and provide support.
To create and secure your account.
To improve the Website through aggregated, non-advertising analytics (with your consent where required).
To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents.
To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
To send service messages and, only with your consent, marketing updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
We process personal data on the basis of your consent or another lawful ground permitted by Data Protection Law, such as compliance with the law and the legitimate uses it recognises. We never use personal data for profiling or targeted advertising directed at children.

4. Finder scans
When you scan a Tag, we record the scan to protect the owner and prevent misuse. The Scan Page shows only limited details chosen by the parent, never a phone number or email address. If you tap “Notify owner”, we alert the parent that their item was found, with the time and any message you add. We do not ask for your name or contact details. We do not collect precise location from your device unless you choose to share it.

You can change your choices through the cookie banner or your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Website works. We do not place analytics or advertising cookies on Scan Pages.

6. Who we share data with
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:
Service providers that host, secure, analyse or support the Website, under written agreements that limit their use of your data.
Payment and logistics partners, where you place an order, only to the extent needed to process payment and deliver it.
The parent, when you notify them about a found item, limited to the scan time and your message.
Authorities, where required by law or court order, or to protect rights and safety.
A successor, if our business is reorganised or transferred, subject to this policy.

7. Storage and international transfers
We store personal data on secure servers in [India / country]. If personal data is transferred outside India, we will do so in line with Data Protection Law and require equivalent protection.

8. How long we keep data
DataRetention

When data is no longer needed, we delete or irreversibly anonymise it.

9. Security
We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, logging, regular testing and staff confidentiality commitments. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals as Data Protection Law requires.

10. Children
The Website is directed to adults. Information about children reaches us only through parents using our services, under the Parent App Privacy Policy and with verifiable parental consent. We do not track, behaviourally monitor or target advertising at children.

11. Your rights
Subject to Data Protection Law, you may:
Request access to a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used.
Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data, and to update or erase data.
Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. This does not affect earlier processing.
Seek redress of grievances with us and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
To exercise a right, email [Privacy Email] from the address linked to your data. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within [30] days.

12. Third-party sites
Links from the Website to other sites are not covered by this policy. Please read their privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy
We will post updates here with a new “Last updated” date and tell you about material changes by notice on the Website or by email.
